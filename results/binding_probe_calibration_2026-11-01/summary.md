# Temporary dynamic probe calibration

The no-resampling probe passes its safety checks in all 16 driven cases. It stops exactly after 80 updates; all retained fields evolve under the unchanged native rules. Two fixed nominal inputs were tested on two archived minimal-V1 states.

Only 8/16 cases resolve both requested core movements (lower input 2/8; higher 6/8). Neither level passes all modes on both states, so no universal repositioning level is selected and no confirmatory binding experiment is run. No dose escalation or relaxed criterion was used.

Descriptively, all tracked positions remain at their release locations for the following 80 updates. Holding one core's nominal direction fixed while changing its partner's gives identical tracked paths in the 16 matched comparisons. The fields remain active globally. These observations resemble individual directional response/pinning in the two cases, not a demonstrated bound assembly or proof against binding generally.
