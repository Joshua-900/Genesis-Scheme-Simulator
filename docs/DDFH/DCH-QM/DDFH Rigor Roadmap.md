✅ **Genesis Scheme (Formal):** Axioms A1–A6, definitions, propositions, pseudocode. Stable skeleton.  
✅ **Genesis Ladder (Formal):** Visual/structural metaphor, tied directly to Genesis Scheme.

**Status:** Stable, frozen, canonical.
