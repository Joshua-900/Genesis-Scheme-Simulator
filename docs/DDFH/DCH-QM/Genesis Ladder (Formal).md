

## Definition

The **Genesis Ladder** is the structural and visual metaphor for the [[Genesis Scheme (Formal)]]. It represents the process of emergence as successive rungs of a ladder, with recursion enabling indefinite ascent.

---

## Structure

- **First Rung (Ground, G):** Unity, indivisible condition (see [[Ground (Formal)]], axioms G1–G5).
- **Second Rung (Substrate, Σ):** Difference; relational 2D encoding surface with distinctions Δ.
- **Third Rung (Bulk, B):** Distinction; emergent 3D volumetric form shaped by lawful mappings Λ.
- **Recursion (ρ):** Each Bulk B may act as a new Ground G′, producing an indefinite ladder of emergence.
- **Collapse:** Bulk → Substrate; volume compresses into surface encoding.

---

## Purpose

- Provides an intuitive, diagrammatic way to represent the Genesis Scheme.
- Makes recursion and collapse visually accessible.
- Serves as a bridge from abstract axioms to concrete instantiations like [[DDFH Core Hypothesis (Formal)]].

---

## Relations

- Diagrammatic representation of the [[Genesis Scheme (Formal)]].
- Rooted in the axioms of [[Ground (Formal)]].
- Instantiated physically in [[DDFH Core Hypothesis (Formal)]].
