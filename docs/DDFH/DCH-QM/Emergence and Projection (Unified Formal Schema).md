1. Unified Operator Framework
    

Both processes can be expressed through a single flow operator Φ acting on the qualitative field (𝒬):

Φ : 𝒬 → g_{ij} \quad \text{with evolution law} \quad ∂_τ g_{ij} = −κ \frac{δ [𝓘 + V(𝒬)]}{δ g_{ij}}

Here:

Emergence: evolution of qualitative order within 𝒬 (intrinsic dynamic).

Projection: translation of that order into measurable geometry (extrinsic expression).

Both are aspects of Φ; the difference lies in the level of constraint applied to the qualitative potential V(𝒬).
