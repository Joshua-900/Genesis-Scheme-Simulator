## Common Notation

- **Σ**: substrate (2D informational manifold), **B**: bulk (3D emergent space)
- **ι : B → Σ** (embedding), **π : Σ → B** (projection)
- **d_Σ(·,·)** / **d_B(·,·)**: distances on Σ / B
- **α(s)**: dimensional accessibility at scale **s**
- **Π**: projection/channel from Σ-dynamics to B-observables
- **S(t)**: deterministic substrate state/evolution
- **D_model**: DOFs assumed by bulk model; **D_access**: physically accessible DOFs
- **I_bulk(V)**, **I_boundary(∂V)**: information content (bulk vs boundary)
- **C(a,b)**: correlation as a function of settings **a,b**
- **λ**: hidden variables defined on Σ
