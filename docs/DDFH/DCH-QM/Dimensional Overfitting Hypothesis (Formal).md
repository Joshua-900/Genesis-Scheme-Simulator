## Definition

The **Dimensional Overfitting Hypothesis (DOH)** proposes that quantum paradoxes and probabilistic formalisms arise because 3D bulk models impose artificial degrees of freedom on systems that are actually constrained to lower-dimensional manifolds (2D or fractional-D). This dimensional misfit forces probability to appear as a compensatory measure.

---

## Core Concept

- **Standard QM assumption:** universal 3D accessibility across all scales.
- **DOH claim:** At quantum scales, systems may be restricted to lower-dimensional relational structures.
- **Result:** 3D models “solve for variables that do not exist,” creating apparent randomness.

---

## Mechanism of Overfitting

- **Superposition:** Misread as multi-state existence; in DOH, it reflects relational ambiguity in inaccessible dimensions.
- **Probability clouds:** Deterministic pathways exist, but are obscured by absent dimensions.
- **Non-locality:** Apparent action-at-a-distance is really geometric adjacency in hidden/constrained dimensions.
- **Wavefunction collapse:** Model correction event — bulk description realigns to observable constraints.

---

## Relation to DDFH

- **DDFH:** Quantum indeterminacy = deterministic 2D substrate compacted into probabilistic 3D bulk.
- **DOH:** The persistence of paradoxes arises from the 3D model itself overfitting compacted dynamics.
- Together: DDFH gives the _mechanism_ (compaction); DOH provides the _meta-critique_ (bulk modeling overreach).

---

## Predictions

- Simplified substrate-aware models (reduced bases, spectral compactifications, dimensional accessibility functions) reproduce quantum laws with _fewer parameters_ than 3D models.
- Deviations from standard QM predictions should cluster in paradoxical regimes (Bell tests, contextuality).
- Collapse like effects align with model correction thresholds, not ontological discontinuities.

---

## Falsifiers

- No compacted/reduced models can reproduce paradoxical features (Bell correlations, contextuality).
- 3D bulk models prove irreducible — all degrees of freedom are necessary to predict experiment.
- No predictive simplifications emerge from dimensional reduction strategies.

---

## Implications

1. **Quantum uncertainty = projection artifact.** Not ontological but descriptive.
2. **Collapse = correction event.** Model self-adjusts when overfitting hits constraints.
3. **Dimensional reduction explains probabilistic formalisms** without invoking fundamental indeterminacy.
4. **Quantum phenomena emerge from mismatched dimensionality,** not spooky metaphysics.

---

## Supporting Analogies

- **Epicycles in Ptolemaic astronomy:** Correct predictions but flawed assumptions, overfitting circular orbits instead of ellipses.
- **2D shadow of a 3D object:** Adding probabilistic fuzz to describe depth instead of acknowledging dimensional loss.

---

## Path Forward

- Develop **Dimensional Accessibility Functions** to model dynamic reduction of degrees of freedom with scale.
- Recalculate QM toy systems (particle-in-a-box, hydrogen atom) under constrained dimensional assumptions.
- Check if anomalies (tunneling rates, decoherence thresholds) align with predicted dimensional transitions.
- Compare efficiency: substrate-aware models vs 3D bulk — are fewer parameters sufficient?
