
This hub note links the three foundational elements of the Genesis framework: Ground, Genesis Scheme, and Genesis Ladder. Together they provide the abstract structure from which instantiations like [[DDFH Core Hypothesis (Formal)]] are derived.

---

## 1. [[Ground (Formal)]]

- **Definition:** Minimal necessary condition of existence, defined axiomatically (G1–G5).
- **Role:** Logical prerequisite; not substance but condition.
- **Provides:** Flows that support distinctions and lawful recursion.

---

## 2. [[Genesis Scheme (Formal)]]

- **Definition:** Abstract process of emergence unfolding from the Ground.
- **Core:** ⟨G, Δ, Λ⟩ triad plus recursion ρ.
- **Axioms:** A1–A6 establish existence, autonomy, duality, transformability, lawfulness, and closure.
- **Yields:** Ordered time (ρ), persistence, emergence, and possibility of universal computation.

---

## 3. [[Genesis Ladder (Formal)]]

- **Definition:** Visual/structural metaphor for the Genesis Scheme.
- **Structure:** Rungs = Ground (G) → Substrate (Σ) → Bulk (B).
- **Recursion:** Each Bulk can become a new Ground, generating ladders upward.
- **Collapse:** Bulk can compress back into Substrate, preserving information.

---

## Relation to [[DDFH Core Hypothesis (Formal)]]

- **DDFH** is a physical instantiation of the Genesis Scheme.
- Substrate = 2D informational surface.
- Bulk = 3D spacetime.
- Quantum paradoxes = artifacts of dimensional compaction.
- Genesis provides the logical scaffolding; DDFH provides the physics-specific realization.
