## Hypothesis Statement

The Deterministic Dimensional Flow Hypothesis (DDFH) proposes that quantum phenomena—such as superposition, entanglement, and probabilistic outcomes—are projection artifacts. These arise when a globally-fixed 3D spatial model is applied to systems whose accessible degrees of freedom dynamically reduce with scale. Apparent randomness in quantum mechanics reflects dimensional blindspots, not ontological indeterminacy.

## Axiomatic Core

- **A1 (Variable Accessibility):** Dimensional accessibility is scale-dependent.
- **A2 (Confinement Effect):** Quantum phenomena emerge when degrees of freedom are constrained.
- **A3 (Classical Emergence):** Classical determinism arises as constraints relax with scale.
- **A4 (Epistemic Probability):** Probabilities reflect projection blindspots, not fundamental randomness.
