**Deterministic Dimensional‑Flow Hypothesis (DDFH) – Quick‑Reference Nugget**

*Core Idea*  
- **Hidden substrate**: a compact manifold \(S\subset\mathbb{R}^{D_{\text{bulk}}}\) (toy model: 2‑D torus).  
- **Deterministic evolution**: a first‑order flow \( \dot X = F(X)\) that conserves a “volume” in \(S\).  
- **Projection**: a deterministic map \(P: S\to\mathbb{R}^{4}\) that turns the hidden state into an observable spacetime event.  
- **Result**: quantum statistics (Born rule, Bell violations up to Tsirelson bound, no‑signalling) + classical general relativity in the \(\tau\to\infty\) limit.
