
## Axioms

- **G1 (Existence).** ∃G such that ¬∃world without G.  
    _(Necessity: G exists in all possible worlds.)_
- **G2 (Indivisibility).** G has no proper parts on which it depends.  
    _(It is seamless, not contingent on composition.)_
- **G3 (Self-sufficiency).** G requires no external conditions for its existence.
- **G4 (Generativity).** Within G, distinctions Δ may arise, yielding dualities.
- **G5 (Lawfulness).** Transitions among distinctions in G follow stable mappings Λ.

## Definition

The **Ground** is the necessary, indivisible, self-sufficient state that contains the potential for distinction and whose transformations obey lawful recursion.
