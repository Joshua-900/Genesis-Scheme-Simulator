
## Definition

**Causal Dynamical Triangulations (CDT)** is a quantum gravity framework that models spacetime as an emergent structure composed of discrete simplicial building blocks (triangles, tetrahedra) while preserving causal structure. CDT demonstrates that spacetime dimensionality is not static but dynamically scales with energy and confinement. Within the Deterministic Dimensional Flow Hypothesis (DDFH), CDT provides theoretical support for the principle that **dimensional accessibility is scale-dependent**—a cornerstone of explaining quantum probabilistic behaviors as projection artifacts of constrained geometric flows.

---

## Core Concept

- Spacetime dimensionality is a dynamic variable, not a fixed backdrop.
- At Planck-scale energies, CDT shows spacetime behaves effectively as 2D.
- At macroscopic scales, spacetime unfolds into the familiar 3+1D structure.
- DDFH extends this insight: quantum behaviors arise when systems interact directly with reduced-dimensional regimes, where relational pathways are compactified or inaccessible.
