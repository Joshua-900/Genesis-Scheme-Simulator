# Structural Binding Response — Milestone

## Question and scope

**Can the existing Genesis structural feedback support a reproducible restoring
response of a multi-vortex configuration, beyond individual pinning or ordinary
field relaxation?**

This is a necessary candidate-level test toward the user-endorsed higher-order
object objective. A positive response alone would not establish an autonomous
object, a universal interaction law, novelty, recursive hierarchy or a 3D bulk.
No missing ingredient is assumed. Annihilation is an interaction; a stationary
vortex is not necessarily inert. Vortices do not by themselves imply binding.

Use the **minimal V1 structural preset** through the existing `V1Adapter`, not
the historical full recipe used in the sandbox. Preserve all V1/V2 engines,
the sandbox, source conventions, historical trackers and old results. Add no
driving, new phase well, prescribed pair potential or object template for this
milestone. A changed-model proposal would be a later, separately justified step.

## What is already known

The closed baseline supports a local structural effect on strict continuation.
The pair forecast study supports a relational candidate class but missed its
fixed object gate. The subsequent compact motion and continuation forecasts
also missed their gates; most surviving anchors had no grid-resolved motion.
Those are inherited results, not unanswered questions to rerun or retune.

The new delta is an intervention on **relative configuration** and its response,
rather than another survival classifier or another structural reset census.

## Stage A: archive readiness — completed

`src/genesis_binding/preflight.py` reads the existing 24 motion archives without
advancing any state. Full initial theta/tau/ux/uy/C fields, finite values and
the selected +1/-1 plaquette charges were verified in all 24 cases. The closed
baseline fingerprints and numerical environment match.

Only seeds 1005 and 3001 meet the initial geometric prerequisite proposed for
radius 3 relocation calibration: pair separation at least8 sites and every other
defect farther than4 sites from both selected cores. This prerequisite checks
room for an intervention; it is not a binding criterion or a new object gate.
Seed 1005 is first by initial geometry and seed order, without future selection.

For context, ten archived pairs have no grid-resolved movement before their
first joint tracking loss; twelve retain both original identities at the end
of the400-update untouched follow-up. Persistence, immobility and tracking loss
are not proofs of binding, inertness or annihilation, respectively.

The selected pairs inhabit populated fields. The two-case clearance result
shows why surrounding-field and lattice-pinning controls matter. It does not
establish that these two cases can be validly relocated or that a broader
population of isolated pairs exists.

## Stage B: intervention calibration — completed, method not accepted

[[Structural Binding Response — Relocation Calibration]] records the frozen
ten-case calibration. Both shams pass; all eight nonsham arms exceed the
declared 25% displacement roundtrip relative L2 limit, with discrepancies
of 26.0%–51.4% inside support. Core motion, full winding maps and protected core
values pass. This is an intervention failure, not a binding result. No tolerance
was relaxed and Stage C was not run. The criteria below remain the original
requirements, not a promise that the failed relocation is usable.

Use the old archives for method development only. Specify and validate a small
local relocation of a defect **together with its retained structural neighborhood**.
Moving theta alone could simply detach a core from its own structural imprint
and measure return to that imprint rather than mutual pair binding.

Completion requires:

1. An explicit interpolation/relocation operation, support, displacement,
   numerical tolerances and boundary treatment recorded before calibration.
   The operation is a prepared counterfactual, not presumed physical motion.
2. Verified intended core displacement and sign, no extra or erased winding
   charges, bounded off-support changes and finite field values. Record
   magnitude/interpolation artifacts in each field and the zero-displacement
   sham. Tracker IDs across the intervention must not be silently assumed.
3. Separable relative stretch/compression and common-translation interventions
   with matched constituent displacement magnitudes. Validate tracker/readout
   resolution and identity assignment before interpreting later trajectories.
4. A recorded feasibility decision. If the operation cannot meet its stated
   criteria, report that method failure; do not interpret failed displacement
   as absent binding or relax tolerances after inspecting a response.

This completed calibration generated no dynamical trajectories.

## Stage B2: temporary dynamic probe — completed

[[Structural Binding Response — Dynamic Probe Calibration]] records the distinct
no-resampling method. A bounded external angular input acts for 80 updates, then
switches off for 80 updates. Native structural updates and locking remain active.
All 16 driven cases pass safety checks. Only 8/16 meet the declared movement
criterion: 2/8 at the lower level and 6/8 at the higher. Neither shared level
calibrates every mode on both archived states, so no level is selected.

Descriptively, positions remain at their release locations throughout follow-up.
Changing the partner's nominal input while holding a core's own direction fixed
leaves its grid-resolved trajectory unchanged in these cases. Global fields
continue evolving. These two development states do not demonstrate a bound
assembly, exclude weak/slow coupling or identify the origin of pinning.
All 60 tests pass. No input escalation, new permanent rule, structural ablation
or fresh-seed response campaign was added.

## Stage C: controlled response — not run

Compare relative stretch and compression with untouched/sham and common
translation arms. Both relative directions matter: simple contraction or mutual
annihilation is not a restoring response around an assembly's configuration.
Use the untouched evolving pair as reference rather than assume its separation
is a stationary equilibrium value.

Record at least: relative-separation deviation from the untouched arm, its
signed recovery, center motion, constituent continuation and winding census,
and changes in all structural fields. Recovery after common translation helps
identify return to a spatial cage. Return alone cannot establish mutual binding.

Include a mechanism comparison that disables the geometry-feedback bundle
consistently, and matched surrounding-field controls. Specify exactly what
initial fields and rules differ. An effect of removing geometry is not by
itself proof that two defects constitute one object.

Before new response collection, freeze the arms, calibrated intervention,
fresh seed/cohort and present-state candidate policy, horizon, sample budget,
coverage requirements, numerical thresholds and interpretation rules. Keep
the old forecast gates unchanged. No confirmatory threshold or sample budget
has been set by this preparation note, and no response experiment has begun.

## Decision at the milestone

- A replicated, mechanism-dependent restoring response beyond the controls
  supports a candidate composite. Next require a consequential collective
  internal state or interaction capability before claiming a higher-order object.
- Consistent pinning, unbound motion, annihilation or no resolved restoration
  narrows the existing model's behavior within the tested scope. Identify the
  missing mechanism before proposing a separate changed-model branch.
- Failed intervention validity, insufficient eligible pairs or ambiguous tracking
  is a method/coverage limit, not a negative scientific result about binding.

The present status is **preflight, relocation and dynamic-probe calibration
complete; no uniformly calibrated displacement level; no confirmatory response
study**. Retain the angular probe as an exploratory intervention. Before another
campaign, identify the proposed collective-cohesion mechanism and a discriminator
against individual response using the existing analyses and recorded traces.
No bound assembly or new permanent force/fuel source has been established.
