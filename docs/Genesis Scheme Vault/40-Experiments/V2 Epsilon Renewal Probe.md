# V2 Epsilon Renewal Probe

## Question

Can slow depletion, transport, and replenishment of potential availability
prevent relation domains from merging indefinitely?

## Mechanism

Epsilon preserves gamma boundaries. It changes the potential-availability
rule: locally coherent actuality consumes availability; availability diffuses
and replenishes slowly. The intended role is to make sustained consolidation
locally self-limiting without imposing a global pulse.

## Result

At 2,000 frames, epsilon generated a population of roughly 23--25 domains in
every calibrated regime, with no dominant box-scale component. At 8,000
frames, the two tested balances both became a single 95%+ domain.

The availability field settled to a nearly constant level while coarsening
continued. Epsilon is therefore a passive resource equilibrium, not a
demonstrated renewal cycle or scale-selection mechanism.

## Next Gate

Do not add a global clock. Any cyclic successor must add a delayed feedback
state that directly affects local consolidation and has a recovery or
refractory phase. It must demonstrate all of the following against gamma,
delta, and epsilon:

1. a bounded late-time domain-area distribution;
2. bounded track areas over the same horizon; and
3. a measured local cycle or renewal event rather than a steady resource
   level.

This is a familiar activator--recovery / activator--inhibitor problem class.
Its use would be a benchmark, not a novelty claim.
