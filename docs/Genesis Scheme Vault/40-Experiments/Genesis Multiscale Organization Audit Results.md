# Genesis Multiscale Organization Audit Results

The first audit is complete. Across 32×32 and 64×64 V1 grids and independent test/replication cohorts, the fixed nested-block representation did **not** clear its predeclared predictive gate over matched planar shells. The supported result is a limit of this representation and protocol, not a rejection of Genesis or all multiscale descriptions.

| Grid | Test loss reduction vs planar | Replication loss reduction vs planar | Gate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 32×32 | +3.00% | +0.39% | Not passed |
| 64×64 | -2.74% | -8.55% | Not passed |

Positive values favor multiscale. Every cohort needed at least 5% gain, a positive paired-seed 95% bootstrap lower bound, and wins in at least 5/6 seeds against all three declared comparators. Both grid sizes had to pass.

Original snapshots show spatial organization relative to shuffled controls, but ordinary local alignment can produce that observation. No holographic bulk was reconstructed.

The run retained 108 seed archives and 1,296 cloned perturbation events across V1, alignment-only and geometry-ablation pipelines at two grid sizes. All 53 checked V1/V2 Python source hashes matched before and after; all 18 tests passed, including original V2 regressions.
