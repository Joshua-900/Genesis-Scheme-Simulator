# V2 Delta Scale Selection Probe

## Question

Can a minimal competing-scale rule prevent gamma's relation domains from
merging indefinitely without destroying potentiality/actuality distinction?

## Mechanism

Delta preserves gamma's boundary mechanism and adds a named
`scale_selection_rule`: actualization aligns with nearby relations while a
weak, repeatedly smoothed relational direction applies counter-torque at a
broader scale. The intended formal role is clear: local coherence may form,
but broad coherence should not absorb every alternative.

## Result

Delta creates a useful middle regime. Weak counter-relation produces many
finite domains at intermediate times; strong counter-relation fragments the
field. Long-horizon runs reject the stronger claim, however: low-counter runs
continued to form a dominant domain around 75--81% of both `N=64` and `N=96`
lattices by frame 8,000.

Delta therefore slows and textures coarsening but does not demonstrate an
intrinsic bounded object scale.

## Next Gate

The next candidate must add renewal through a defined state process rather
than another static spatial correction. A legitimate resource or inhibition
mechanism must state:

1. what local process accumulates or depletes;
2. its recovery and spatial transport timescales; and
3. why those dynamics counter domain merger.

It must then beat delta on late-time bounded-area distributions and
bounded-track survival across lattice size and observer-cutoff controls.
