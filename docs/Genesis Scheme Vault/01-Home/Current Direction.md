# Current Direction

## Purpose

This note is the authoritative answer to: where did the project stop, what is currently trusted, and what should happen next? Keep it short enough to read before resuming work and update it whenever the active milestone or a project-level decision changes.

## Current Theory State

The current canonical formulation is Unity-first: the Genesis Scheme describes a progression from unity through distinction, relation, emergence, stabilization, and recursion.

Potentiality-Actuality is a draft v2 research direction. It proposes that potentiality and actuality are co-primitive, with differentiation as their irreducible relation. Under this interpretation, unity is either undifferentiated potential or achieved coherence. This is not yet canonical and has not changed the meaning of existing simulations.

## Current Implementation State

- `src/genesis_grid/` is the current v1 experimental engine.
- The v1 engine models an orientation field with modular rules for alignment, compression, curvature, and optional mechanisms.
- Legacy scalar and reaction-diffusion engines remain baselines and historical prototypes.
- The `GenesisEngine` interface and registry permit a future v2 engine with a different state representation without rewriting v1.
- `src/genesis_v2_integrated/` is the active v2 bridge experiment. It retains
  V1's tension-to-structure loop while adding distinct potentiality, actuality,
  and relation state. Earlier alpha through zeta packages remain recorded
  exploratory prototypes, not the active continuation path.

## Current Evidence State

- The square-lattice multi-seed measurement is biased and does not yet establish spontaneous symmetry breaking. See the linked finding below.
- Existing simulations are evidence about their specific models, not evidence for metaphysical claims.
- The core dynamical ingredients have established precedents. See [[Genesis Scheme Prior Art Review]].

   and hypotheses in isolated modules/adapters; do not rewrite either baseline

   pulsed coupling require a stated role and a discriminating counterfactual;
   they are not post-hoc rescue parameters.

## Earlier V2/Object Work Queue — Historical Context

The following records the prior programme and its successive results. Its
embedded “next” suggestions are historical, superseded by the active
generative review above. They do not commission another experiment.

The relation-memory sweep and V1 geometry ablation are complete. Relation
memory raised compression without improving persistence, and population-level
geometry changes did not explain candidate counts. A 30-seed matched local
perturbation did establish a narrower causal lead: weakening the
candidate-associated structural neighborhood reduced median strict continuation
from 22 to 5 samples, while the same-shape background intervention matched the
untouched control at 22. The 30-seed P/A bridge replication preserved this local
structural effect (`p` approximately `0.000107`) while P remained distinct from
A. It did not yet show a new capability beyond V1. Next: apply a matched
potential-only perturbation at the selected candidate neighborhood and equal-
shape background, leaving structural state untouched, to test whether P is a
causal degree of freedom rather than a decorative input. The first 30-seed,
90-degree P-rotation test was not resolved (`p = 0.227`). Before another V2
mechanism is added, measure whether the existing P-to-A coupling changes any
predeclared high-compression observable relative to V1 parity. That audit is
now complete: P-to-A reliably changed P/A alignment, mean tension, and vortex
count, while global actual-order and mean compression were unresolved. P has
therefore earned a field-level dynamical role, but not an object-level claim.
The matched 30-seed propagation test is also complete: a local P rotation did
not yield a candidate-specific far-field response in A, tension, or compression
over 250 frames. The current P bridge has a system-level role, but has not
shown a locally propagating structural role for tracked candidates. Next:
the 20-seed P-to-A dose-response sweep is complete. P/A alignment and tension
rise monotonically with gain, while compression and vortex count rise overall
and actual global order declines at the strongest tested coupling. The
validated local structural continuation effect persists at every gain, but its
size has no monotonic gain trend. P is therefore a controllable field-level
parameter that does not replace the V1 structural mechanism. Next: map the
apparent high-gain transition between `0.10` and `0.20` with the mechanism held
fixed; do not add a new coupling before distinguishing a regime boundary from a
smooth tradeoff.

The object-level forecast gate is also complete for the V1 candidate class.
Across two independent ten-seed cohorts, relational pair state beat an
equal-information local-field survival model in 19 of 20 held-out seeds, but
its combined Brier reduction was 4.25%, below the predeclared 5% threshold.
The supported claim is therefore a reproducible **relational candidate class**,
not an autonomous higher-order object. Next object-specific task: test whether
a coarse pair state supports a transferable short-horizon transition model for
position, length, and survival better than an equally sized local-field
transition model. Do not lower the forecast gate or tune features to force a
claim.

The individual-vortex object test has now identified the correct V1 object
level. Across ten seeds, localized integer-winding vortices form a recurrent,
long-lived class; across twenty matched smooth-core perturbations, strict
tracker-ID-and-charge continuation matched the untouched control. V1 therefore

### Completed Stabilization Derivation — 2026-10-31

[[V1 Stabilization — Mechanism and Discriminating Prediction]] provides a
source-based analysis of the existing structural preset. Locking is finite
angular braking (minimum mobility 0.04); changing its strength below one leaves
full static configurations unchanged, though stability and basins can change.
A full curvature-off static state requires zero net alignment torque. A
maintained nonzero-torque structure would therefore need the separate curvature
force balance. Its existence and stability are not yet established. The derived
10-fold braking threshold is |detF|<=0.06351, and direct homogeneous retained
state decays on documented update scales; shell feedback can renew geometry.
Transcribed equations match single updates of all 24 archived motion starting
states to below 1e-15. This inherited the closed baseline and launched no new
trajectory campaign. The next mathematical target is existence/stability of a
nonuniform full-state balance, rather than another parameter search.

### Completed Equilibrium Study — 2026-10-31

[[V1 Equilibria — Search and Shell Balance]] records 54 frozen small-grid
attempts: 20 accepted numerical outcomes, all near ordinary alignment
stationarity, and no qualifying nonzero-torque witness. No stable-witness
larger-grid tests were triggered. This is a bounded negative search, not a

nonlinear shell/compression balance remains an open mathematical target under
