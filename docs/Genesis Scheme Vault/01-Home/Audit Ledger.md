## Stabilization derivation coverage

[[V1 Stabilization — Mechanism and Discriminating Prediction]] distinguishes
positive angular mobility from restoring force balance. At the tested preset,
locking leaves at least 4% response and does not add full static configurations
for locking strength below one; stability and basins may still change. Without
curvature, a full static state requires zero net alignment torque. These results
are conditional on the documented preset and bounds, not a proof that full V1
contains stable nonzero-torque structures. The next open mathematical scope is
existence and stability of such structures. [[V1 Equilibria — Search and Shell Balance]]
records a completed bounded search, which did not supply a witness, and a
necessary shell-curvature torque budget. The nonlinear shell branch, dynamic
states and larger domains are not settled by that result. See the derivation for
verification and the derived memory/response thresholds; do not repeat these
algebra checks as a new discovery.
