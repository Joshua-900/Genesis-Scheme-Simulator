# Vortex Dynamics — Candidate Routes to Higher-Order Objects

## Accepted objective

The user endorses demonstrating one reproducible, nontrivial higher-order
structure beyond an isolated vortex, and showing what its organization enables
beyond simpler controls. Metaphysical intuition guides the construction; neither
physical realism, attractive images nor perpetual activity is required for that
first result. The sandbox remains a needed exploratory instrument.

## Existing mechanisms worth knowing

These are mechanism references, not results already demonstrated by Genesis.

| Family | Established result | Important ingredient and transfer limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complex Ginzburg–Landau | Bound states of interacting spiral waves, including multiple bound separations | Complex amplitude and phase dynamics support wave-mediated interactions. An orientation vortex in Genesis is not automatically a wave-emitting spiral. |
| Gross–Pitaevskii | Vortex dipoles that propagate as solitary structures or annihilate, depending on the prepared field profile | Complex wave dynamics differ from Genesis's alignment/retained-geometry map. Winding alone does not supply the motion law. |
| Rabi-coupled two-component condensates | Bound vortex molecules and molecular lattices | Binding depends on an explicit inter-component coupling. It is not produced merely by placing two arbitrary theta fields together. |
| Active nematics | Coupled orientation/flow defects and continuing defect creation/annihilation in active turbulence | Active stresses and flow supply dynamics absent from the current Genesis preset. Sustained activity does not itself demonstrate recursively assembling objects. |

Primary sources:

- [Aranson, Kramer & Weber, 1993: spiral-wave bound states](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.47.3231).
- [Rorai, Sreenivasan & Fisher, 2012: propagating/annihilating dipoles](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.6389).
- [Uranga & Lamacraft, 2018: vortex molecules](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.09912).
- [Spontaneous self-constraint in active nematic flows, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-023-02336-5).

The table summarizes specific reported results; it is not an exhaustive
literature or novelty review. These precedents mean that a bound vortex pair
alone would not establish novelty. A distinctive Genesis mechanism would need
its own comparison and evidence.

## What fuel means here

Genesis has no established conserved energy or thermodynamic fuel variable.
Its tension update, geometry damping, turning term and structural feedback
are supplied algorithms without a derived complete energy budget. Therefore
apparent settling cannot yet be attributed quantitatively to fuel exhaustion.

Separate three questions: how existing differences evolve; whether the rules
generate continuing instabilities; and whether interactions can bind elementary
structures into a new collective unit. A failure of binding is not necessarily
a shortage of activity. More driving can produce more motion without a new
level of organization. A stationary composite, if stable and causally coherent,
can also satisfy the next object milestone.

## The Genesis-specific candidate

The nearest candidate is a vortex assembly bound or organized through its
retained tension/displacement/compression environment. This builds on the
already demonstrated local structural influence on continuation. It does not
assume that nearby defects already form a molecule or that the retained
structural field mediates an established general interaction law.

The archived pair forecasts and vortex-motion studies must be inherited:
relational pair information had predictive value but missed its fixed gate;
the subsequent compact motion/continuation predictors also failed their gates.
Those outcomes constrain the proposal and are not to be repeated or retuned
under a new name. Before a new experiment, identify whether missing evidence
concerns binding/restoring response, collective internal state, or transferable
interaction behavior, and check what the existing archives can already answer.

An assembly counts as a higher-order candidate if its internal organization
persists and affects its collective response. Several tracked vortices in one
box, or several defects pinned independently to the lattice, are insufficient.
If an isolated changed-model branch becomes necessary, change one named
interaction mechanism, preserve V1/V2, and keep external reference models
distinct from Genesis-derived results.

## Recursive hierarchy and the bulk vision

The proposed progression is field → defects → coherent assemblies → interacting
assemblies. Every arrow needs a new result; one successful composite does not
guarantee an unlimited hierarchy. A collective description should retain state
that predicts interactions, rather than merely rename groups of elementary
defects. A useful next level may have different effective dynamics.

Higher organizational order is not another spatial direction. The proposed
3D bulk remains a separate long-range conjecture requiring independently
accessible locations and relationships with volume-like geometry. Neither
vortex molecules nor repeated coarse-graining alone establishes it. The accepted
object milestone is worthwhile even if the bulk conjecture never succeeds.

No new model, driving term, parameter sweep or trajectory campaign was started
