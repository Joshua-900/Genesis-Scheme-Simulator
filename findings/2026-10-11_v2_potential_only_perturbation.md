# Potential-Only Rotation Was Not a Resolved Continuation Effect

Thirty fixed seeds used the high-compression P/A bridge. Structural state was
untouched. Potential direction was rotated by 90 degrees inside either the
selected candidate neighborhood or an equal-radius background mask.

| Branch | End survival | Median continuation |
|---|---:|---:|
| Untouched | 7 / 30 | 8 |
| Candidate-local P rotation | 5 / 30 | 6 |
| Background P rotation | 7 / 30 | 8 |

Candidate-local continuation was lower in 11 seeds, equal in 11, and higher in
8. The paired one-sided Wilcoxon result was `p = 0.227`. Background and
untouched matched exactly in all 30 seeds.

This limits the tested 90-degree P-direction perturbation; it does not show
that P is decorative or irrelevant. Before another V2 mechanism is added,
measure whether the existing P-to-A coupling changes any predeclared
high-compression observable relative to V1 parity.
