# Genesis as a Generative System: A Concrete Texture Lead

Date: 2026-10-31. Study: `generative_review_2026-10-31`.

The user's current objective is a distinctive, useful generative system;
physics is speculative motivation. Earlier scientific negative results remain
intact but are not acceptance tests for that objective.

Six existing configurations were compared at N64 for 8000 updates on seeds 1/7/42,
with identical initial angles, fixed rendering, and a common local pulse.
No parameter search or new dynamical mechanism was added.

The historical full recipe consistently forms larger connected mosaic patches
and retains modest late spatial change. Its geometry-disabled control retains
a fine mosaic and nearly stops: late phase-aligned RMS change per100 updates
is 0.0113–0.0194 with geometry versus <2e-9 without it. Final neighbor disagreement
is 0.2521–0.2778 versus 0.5376–0.5627. These are three-seed ranges, not inferential
statistics. Geometry is disabled as a bundle, so its components are not isolated.

Minimal V1 and its simpler references remain visually similar broad smoothing
fields, despite differing trajectories and structural diagnostics. V2 produces
a visibly finer texture; it also has additional initialized source state, so
this cross-version comparison is descriptive. The historical full recipe's
four preferred orientations and triad templates are explicitly supplied.

Late activity does not establish indefinite renewal or Life-like mobile objects.
Both historical variants retain a localized response 400 updates after the same
pulse; the geometry-disabled variant has the larger global difference here.
There is no demonstrated editability advantage for geometry in that one probe.

Recommendation: retain the full historical recipe as a texture-generator lead.
The next useful milestone would be a small creative sandbox with unchanged
rules, judged by whether its patterns and editing are worth using. This review
does not establish global novelty or a strong case for an open-ended research
campaign.

All 41 tests pass, including parity against the original GUI update. Preserved
V1/V2 sources, legacy source and baseline evidence fingerprints remain unchanged.
