# A clean temporary probe moves some defects; these pairs show no resolved recoil

Date: 2026-11-01. Scope: B2 method calibration on the same two archived minimal-V1
states, not a confirmatory binding experiment.

The probe adds a bounded angular transport-like torque for 80 updates, with a
sin-squared envelope and fixed radius 3 supports, then switches off for 80 updates.
Native compression locking applies without compensation. Tension, displacement
and compression follow the unchanged engine; no field is resampled. Two nominal
levels 0.05/0.10 were fixed before collection. Each state has untouched reference,
stretch/compression and common +/- translation prescriptions. Inputs have matched
nominal profiles, not guaranteed equal realized motion or torque exposure.

## Fixed calibration outcome

All 16 driven cases pass the declared safety criteria: selected tracker identities
continue during the pulse, vortex census and tracked births do not exceed the
untouched reference, fields remain finite, direct external angular increments
are bounded, and removal is exact. The first unforced step matches a native
step from the identical perturbed state bitwise. Zero input also reproduces the
old untouched position trajectories.

Only 8/16 cases meet the predefined movement requirement. The lower level passes
2/8; the higher passes 6/8. Seed 1005 at the higher level passes all four modes;
seed 3001 fails stretch and negative common translation because its positive
core does not move in the negative axial direction. No level passes every mode
on both states. No dose was increased or criterion relaxed.

The implementation is a clean controlled angular disturbance under these checks;
it is not a uniformly calibrated two-core repositioning instrument.

## Descriptive release observations

After cutoff, both selected positions remain at their release locations at every
one of the 80 follow-up updates in all 16 cases. There is no grid-resolved recoil
within this window. Global theta and retained fields continue changing, so this
does not establish a full-state fixed point.

The four input modes form a two-core sign factorial. Holding one core's nominal
direction fixed while changing the partner's direction gives identical tracked
positions throughout the 160-update trajectory in all 16 matched comparisons.
Actual delivered torque need not be identical because its value depends on the
local field and native locking. This post-collection descriptive comparison is
not a revised acceptance gate or a general zero-interaction theorem.

These observations are consistent with directional local mobility/pinning in
the sampled regime. They do not show a spring-like collective restoring response
or demonstrate a higher-order object. They do not distinguish lattice, background
or self-imprint pinning, and cannot exclude weak, sub-grid or longer-time coupling.
The old states were method-development cases, not fresh confirmatory cohorts.

## Decision

Retain the temporary probe as an exploratory intervention. Keep the stricter
calibration outcome: no shared input level qualifies for the original controlled
repositioning requirement. Stage C is not launched. The calibration does not
justify escalating the dose or asserting that an ingredient is missing.

The next useful question is which existing mechanism could produce demonstrable
collective cohesion beyond these individual responses. Inspect these trajectories
and inherited analyses before another parameter or seed campaign. The probe adds
no permanent rule, fuel budget or spontaneous object-generating mechanism.

All 60 tests pass. The failed relocation calibration, old motion archives and
closed V1/V2 baseline remain unchanged.
