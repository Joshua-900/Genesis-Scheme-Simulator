# P/A Dynamics Preserve the Validated Local Structural Effect

## Headline

In the 30-seed high-compression V1-derived bridge, independent P-to-A dynamics
preserved the causal local structural continuation effect. The model is now a
genuine continuity bridge: it retains the validated V1 mechanism while keeping
potentiality distinct from actuality. It has not yet shown a new object-level
capability beyond V1.

## Design

Both conditions used the same `kappa=0.1` structural substrate, candidate
selection, radius-5 structural weakening, and strict constituent-ID
continuation measure. The P/A condition added only independent potential
transport, source restoration, and P-to-A influence. Each condition used the
same fixed 30 seeds and candidate-neighborhood, background, and untouched
branches.

## Results

| Condition | Untouched median | Candidate-neighborhood median | Background median | Target lower than background |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| V1 parity | 20 | 4 | 20 | 20 / 30 |
| Independent P-to-A | 8 | 2 | 8 | 21 / 30 |

For the local effect, paired target-versus-background Wilcoxon results were
`p = 0.000106` in V1 parity and `p = 0.000107` with P-to-A dynamics. The
paired difference between untouched P-to-A and untouched parity continuation
was not resolved at this sample size (`p = 0.115`).

## Interpretation

The same-shape background branch matched untouched exactly in both conditions.
Therefore the structural perturbation remains locally specific after adding P/A
dynamics. P-to-A does not erase the causal V1 structural lead. The lower raw
P-to-A medians are not enough to claim that potentiality harms persistence;
that comparison remains uncertain.

## Next Test

Perform a matched **potential-only** perturbation at the selected candidate
neighborhood and equal-shape background, without weakening structural state.
That is the direct test of whether P adds a causal degree of freedom beyond the
V1 substrate.
