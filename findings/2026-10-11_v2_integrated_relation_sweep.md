# Relation-Memory Strength Sweep Does Not Stabilize Candidate Pairs

## Headline

Across the predeclared relation-memory tension strengths `0`, `0.1`, `0.2`,
`0.4`, and `0.8`, compression rose but persistent vortex-pair candidates did
not. This is a negative result for relation memory alone as the missing
stabilizing link in the V1-derived V2 bridge.

## Fixed Conditions

Each condition used five seeds, `N=64`, 2,000 frames, and 25-frame tracking
samples. The P-to-A bridge and V1 census-compatible rule pipeline were held
fixed. No additional rule or parameter was introduced.

## Median Final Measurements

| Relation-memory tension | P/A alignment | Mean compression | Persistent pairs | Median pair age |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.0 | 0.2733 | 0.00000051 | 117 | 25 |
| 0.1 | 0.2682 | 0.00000114 | 117 | 25 |
| 0.2 | 0.2679 | 0.00000101 | 117 | 25 |
| 0.4 | 0.2660 | 0.00088121 | 120 | 25 |
| 0.8 | 0.2512 | 0.00231378 | 119 | 25 |

## Interpretation

The state did not collapse into P/A alignment, and stronger memory did produce
more compression. But persistence remained effectively unchanged; the small
variation around 117--120 pairs is not evidence of stabilization. There is no
selected relation-memory strength to carry forward as a success.

## Next Test

Directly ablate V1's geometry/compression coupling while holding its other
candidate-producing settings fixed. Before V2 imports more structural machinery,
the project must establish whether that V1 pathway causally changes candidate
lifetimes or only accompanies them.
